Who We Are
The problem we solve
Your accountant does the books.
Nobody owns what sits between.
Most businesses at the $1M to $15M stage have accounting covered. They may even have a fractional CFO advising at the top. But the layer in between - the data infrastructure, workflows, reporting systems, and validation processes - is often nobody's job. The result? Reports that take too long, numbers that nobody fully trusts, and a finance function that is always catching up instead of looking ahead.
Where Finology Nexus came from
Our founder spent years inside enterprise finance environments, including S&P Global, building the exact kind of infrastructure growing businesses rarely have access to. Automated validation pipelines. Clean data architecture. Reporting systems that actually run on schedule. We built Finology Nexus to make that same capability available to businesses that need it without needing a team of 20 to get there.

Our Team
Credentials
Enterprise expertise.
SMB focus.
Founding background
Built inside the institutions that set the benchmark
Our team brings direct experience from S&P Global and leading US-based financial intelligence companies. We have built data pipelines, automated workflows pulling from Moody's, Fitch, and S&P, cut daily validation time from 90 minutes to 15, and driven 99.5% output accuracy across all client deliverables. This is not theoretical. We have done it at scale.
You always know who is doing the work
Every engagement is led by our founding team directly. There is no account management layer, no handoff to junior staff after the contract is signed. The person who scopes the project delivers it. That accountability is intentional.
How we fit alongside your controller or CFO
We work in the infrastructure layer, not the strategy layer. Our outputs feed your controller or CFO, not around them. We define clear handoffs from day one and never step into a role that is already owned.

How We Work
Our process
Simple, structured,
no surprises.
1
Discovery call
A 45-minute conversation to understand what is broken, what tools you are running, and what outcomes matter most. No quote before we understand the real problem.
2
Scoped proposal with Statement of Work
Clear deliverables, realistic timelines, and fixed fees. A formal SOW so both sides know exactly what is expected from the start.
3
30-day build phase
We map workflows, identify bottlenecks, and build the infrastructure. Ends with full documentation and a clear handover.
4
Ongoing support
Monthly retainer or project-by-project, covered by defined SLAs. You know exactly what to expect and when.
Typical investment range
For businesses between $1M to $15M, retainer engagements typically start from $800/month, depending on workflow complexity, reporting scope, and automation depth. Fixed-scope projects typically start from $2,000, scoped to deliverables rather than hours.
